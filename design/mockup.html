    Notes for the owner

    Choices in this mockup you may want to change. Everything here is easy to adjust.

    • Color thresholds. Green is 70% and up, amber is 40% to 69%, grey is under 40%. These are placeholders. Tell me the cutoffs you would use.
    • Columns shown by default. Right now all eight show. On a phone each card becomes a small block. Cash, credit and market price could be hidden behind a "More" link to keep it calmer.
    • Cash or credit as the default. The plan starts on Cash. The percent column always compares cash to market price, and ranking is the same either way.
    • The EX and VG estimates. 80% and 60% of the NM price are guesses, marked as such in Settings. Please check them against CK's grading page before anyone relies on them.
    • "Back on the buylist" alerts. They compare with the previous price update only. A friend who updates rarely will see everything that came back since then, and a friend who updates every day will see only the last day's changes. The very first update has nothing to compare with, so it shows no alert.
    • Page size and remembered step. The cards table shows 100 rows per page. The tool reopens on the step you were last on, and on Step 1 until cards are added.
    • Replace and hand-added cards. "Replace what's there" swaps out only the cards that came from the earlier import. Cards you added by hand are always kept, and the screen says so next to the choice. If you would rather have Replace clear everything, it is a one-line change, but friends could lose cards they typed in.
    • Export My Collection and Import My Collection. The exported file is the friend's own backup of their binder, and also how friends can share a list with each other. Import takes only files made by Export My Collection. It asks before replacing anything and can be undone. The 14-day reminder to export is a gentle line they can dismiss; 14 days is a guess.
    • Automatic saving. The binder is kept on each friend's PC after every change, shown by the quiet "Saved on this PC" note. Export is a copy, not a step.
    • Adding by hand. A card added with "+ Add a card" is always added as Near Mint, and the same printing added twice is joined into one row. Condition, quantity and removal are fixed in the table afterwards.
    • Card pictures. The hover picture is a drawn placeholder here because the mockup has no network. The real tool loads each picture from Scryfall by its card id, and shows nothing if a picture does not load.
    • Small cards. There is no minimum price. A $0.01 card goes in the plan if it ranks high enough or is needed to fill a bag.
    Prices: Card Kingdom's buylist and TCGplayer market (via Scryfall). Not affiliated with Card Kingdom.
