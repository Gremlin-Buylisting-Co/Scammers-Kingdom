## 1.0.0

First version.

- Three steps, one per page: add cards, your binder, plan a shipment.
- Import a shoptcg.online export (replace or add), or add a card by hand with a search over Card Kingdom's list and a card picture on hover.
- The binder is fully editable (quantity, condition, printing, remove, select several), saves at once and has Undo for the last 20 changes.
- Export My Collection and Import My Collection, with a reminder after 14 days without an export.
- Card Kingdom's buylist prices in cash and credit, market prices from Scryfall, CK % of market with colours, and EX / VG estimates.
- Cards you import or add are looked up on Scryfall right away (only the new ones), so they show their prices without pressing Update prices a second time.
- Alerts when a card of yours is back on Card Kingdom's buylist.
- Plan a shipment of 50-card bags, cash or credit, with the bag fee, a CSV spreadsheet and a file for Card Kingdom's CSV import.
- A "Sell on CK" link for each card CK is buying.
- A line in the header when a newer version is published.
